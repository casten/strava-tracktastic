Tracktastic for Strava

Open a Strava activity to switch it on or off for that page.

Longer distances (like the activity total) are left as-is. Changes reload the Strava tab.

A track workout is detected from 3+ fast laps at track distances (200, 400, 800 m…) with recovery laps, or a title like "8x400" or "Track".
